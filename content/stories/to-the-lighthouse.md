Trip to Austrian Neusiedlersee and back (897km in two days)

Free weekend and end of summer. We checked the weather app and the forecast was clear – the north of the Czech Republic will be covered in clouds and rain. So our decision was to do a trip to the south.

![Two Harley-Davidson motorcycles at a petrol station](/media/to-the-lighthouse/DSCF45881-scaled.jpg)

## CRAZY COLD

Well, that was a mistake. All day long we were riding a few minutes behind the rain clouds. It was cold and even the coke was freezing. Luckily the engine was quite hot all the way!

## GABČÍKOVO (SK)

The booking app came in handy. Lake Neusiedl was either fully booked or crazy overpriced. So we checked what was available within a 50km radius. And there it was. A houseboat! It was called Modrá čajka and the experience was pretty awesome! Restaurant with tasty local food and bar full of whisky and rum. So we went in for a couple of shots and just relaxed for a bit.

![Houseboat lit at night on the water at Gabčíkovo](/media/to-the-lighthouse/DSCF48291-scaled.jpg)

![Rope ladder in a small houseboat cabin](/media/to-the-lighthouse/DSCF48141-scaled.jpg)

Gabčíkovo is a waterworks on the Danube in Bratislava and south-east of it in the Trnava region, in southern Slovakia near the border with Hungary. About 1 km south of the village of Gabčíkovo, after which the plant is named, a dam with a hydroelectric power station was built. The resulting reservoir covers an area of 46 km², includes a group of 12 islands (Benko’s Islands) and extends into the Bratislava region. It is situated at an altitude of 131 metres.

![Car driving along the lit dam road at Gabčíkovo at night](/media/to-the-lighthouse/DSCF48551-scaled.jpg)

![Concrete edge and debris in the channel of the Gabčíkovo dam](/media/to-the-lighthouse/DSCF48411-scaled.jpg)

![Light trails of a passing car on the dam road at night](/media/to-the-lighthouse/DSCF48431-scaled.jpg)

![Gabčíkovo ship lock with cranes under street lamps at night](/media/to-the-lighthouse/DSCF48591-scaled.jpg)

It was quite surreal to explore the place in the dark. Even after listening to the Harley engine rumble all day, this place was quite noisy. The architecture is brutal and somehow breathtaking.
